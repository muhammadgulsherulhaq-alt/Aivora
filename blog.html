AI Guides & Resources

Understand AI tools, explore their practical uses, and learn how to choose the right tools for your creative and everyday tasks.

1. How to Choose the Right AI Tool

AI tools can help with writing, image creation, video editing, audio production, research and many other tasks. However, the most popular tool is not always the best choice for every user.

Start with your actual task

First, decide what you want to accomplish. Someone creating social media videos may need a video editor, while a student organizing notes may benefit more from a writing or productivity assistant.

Compare the important features

  • Check whether the tool supports your required task.
  • Look for a free plan or trial before paying.
  • Review export options and supported file formats.
  • Check usage limits, credits and subscription costs.
  • Read the provider's privacy and data policies.

Test before committing

Try a small, realistic task before choosing a subscription. Compare the output quality, ease of use and time required. This helps you decide whether a tool genuinely fits your needs.

2. How AI Writing Tools Can Improve Your Workflow

AI writing tools can assist with brainstorming, outlining, drafting and improving written material. They are most useful when treated as assistants rather than replacements for human judgment.

Useful writing tasks

  • Generating ideas for articles and social posts.
  • Creating an outline before writing a long document.
  • Improving clarity and sentence structure.
  • Summarizing notes that you have permission to use.
  • Adapting a draft for a particular audience.

Give clear instructions

A useful prompt explains the topic, audience, purpose, preferred tone and desired format. For example, instead of asking for general content about technology, specify whether you need a beginner-friendly explanation, a comparison or a checklist.

Review the final result

AI-generated text can contain incorrect facts, outdated information or unnatural wording. Verify important claims, add your own knowledge and edit the result before publishing.

3. A Beginner's Guide to AI Image and Video Tools

AI image and video tools can help creators develop visual concepts, prepare social media content and experiment with different creative styles. Each platform offers different features and limitations.

Understand the difference

Image-generation tools create or modify still visuals. Video-generation tools can produce moving scenes, while AI-assisted video editors may help with captions, trimming, background removal and other editing tasks.

What should you compare?

  • Image resolution and video output quality.
  • Available editing and customization options.
  • Watermarks and export restrictions.
  • Free credits and monthly usage limits.
  • Commercial-use permissions and licensing terms.

Use AI responsibly

Check the platform's rules before using generated content commercially. Avoid misleading viewers, respect copyright and obtain permission before using other people's personal images or recordings.
