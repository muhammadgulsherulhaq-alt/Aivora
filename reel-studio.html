Free Reel & Shorts Maker

Turn your own long videos into vertical 9:16 shorts, or into a Twitter-style video with a rounded video box, channel header and hook caption. It runs inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Pick a video to start. The video is processed on your phone and never uploaded. Keep the screen on while it renders (it runs in real time).

How to use it

1. Save the long video to your phone or computer. 2. Pick it above and set the start and end time of the part you want. 3. Choose the 9:16 Shorts tab, or the Twitter-style tab and add your channel picture, name and hook caption. 4. Press Make video, wait for it to finish (it records in real time), then save the result.

Good to know

Keep the screen on while the video renders. Use only videos you own or have permission to edit and share. The tool does not download videos from other websites. Your video and picture are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to AIVORA.

About the AI captions

The first time you use AI captions, a small speech model is downloaded and then runs on your own device. Your video is not uploaded. It works best for clear speech in English; Urdu and Hindi are experimental. Always check the captions before posting.

Which file will I get?

Most phones and browsers save an MP4 file. Some browsers can only create WebM, which still plays on most devices. The original audio of your video is kept.
