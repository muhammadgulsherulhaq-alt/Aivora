How to use it

1. Select your video. 2. Choose start and end time. 3. Select Shorts or Twitter-style. 4. Add your channel details. 5. Save your channel profile if you want to reuse it. 6. Press Make video and save the result.

Good to know

Keep your screen on while the video renders. Use videos you own or have permission to edit. Your videos and channel pictures are processed on your device.

AI captions

The first time you use AI captions, a speech model is downloaded. It then runs locally on your device. Always check generated captions before publishing.

Which file will I get?

Most browsers save MP4. Some browsers may create WebM. Both formats are supported by many modern video players.
