THE AIVORA BEGINNER'S GUIDE

A Simple Guide to Choosing AI Tools

Artificial intelligence tools can help people complete creative, educational and everyday tasks. From writing and image creation to video planning and productivity, different tools serve different purposes. This guide will help you understand the main categories and choose tools according to your needs.

What Are AI Tools?

AI tools are software applications that use artificial intelligence to assist with specific tasks. They can help generate ideas, draft text, create images, process audio, summarize information and support coding. Some tools create new content from written instructions, while others help improve existing work. AI output should always be reviewed for accuracy, originality and quality before you use it.

How to Choose a Free AI Tool

Start by identifying the task you want to complete. Compare tools based on their features, ease of use, output quality and available free plan. Some providers offer limited daily credits, restricted features, watermarked downloads or paid upgrades. Always check the provider's current pricing and terms because free-plan conditions can change.

Before uploading files, check the service's privacy policy. Avoid sharing passwords, confidential documents or sensitive personal information with tools unless you understand how the provider handles that data.

Explore Different AI Tool Categories

AI Video Tools: These services can assist with video planning, editing, captions, animation and video generation. Check whether a tool creates a finished video or only helps prepare scripts and concepts.

AI Image Tools: Image tools can help create illustrations, design concepts, social media graphics and visual assets. Review download limits, image quality and usage rights.

AI Audio Tools: Audio services may support transcription, voice generation, sound editing and other audio tasks. Check supported languages and permitted uses.

AI Writing Tools: Writing assistants can help with brainstorming, outlining, proofreading and editing. Review and fact-check generated text before publishing it.

Productivity Tools: These tools can assist with planning, summarizing and organizing everyday work.

Developer Tools: AI coding assistants can explain code, suggest improvements and help identify errors. Always test and review code before using it in a project.

How to Use AIVORA

Browse the categories on AIVORA or use the search feature to find tools related to your task. Open the provider's website to check its latest features, pricing and terms. Compare suitable options and try them with a simple, non-sensitive example before choosing one.

AIVORA is an independent discovery directory. Third-party tools are operated by their respective providers, and their features and availability may change.

What Can AIVORA AI Studio Do?

AIVORA AI Studio currently helps users prepare video concepts by entering a topic and selecting settings such as duration, aspect ratio and style. It does not currently generate or export a finished video file. The concept can be used as a starting point when planning a video project.

Use Artificial Intelligence Responsibly

AI-generated information can contain mistakes. Verify important facts and use reliable sources when accuracy matters. Respect copyright, privacy and the terms of each service. Do not use AI output to mislead people or misrepresent someone else's work.
